========== v1.0.0 ==========
IMPLEMENTED 3 TIERS OF CHESTS OBTAINED BY KILLING MONSTERS
* Funcion as Global Rewards (awarded directly into your inventory by killing monsters). 
* The tier of the chests (quality/rarity of its rewards) are directly related to the level of monsters you kill.
* All the equipment you get from these chests have 4 additional random options, divided by:

1) 1st bonus is always attribute-related (STR/AGI/VIT/INT/DEX/LUK)
2) 2nd bonus is always a offensive stat (ATK/ATK M/Long Ranged Dmg) 
3) 3rd bonus is always a defensive stat (HP/SP/FLEE/DEF/MDEF)  - SP is the exception if you don't consider mages
4) 4th bonus is  almost always a utility stat focused on increasing you power(HIT/ASPD/CRIT/Fixed and Variable Cast Time Reduction/After Cast Delay Reduction) - it can have a special roll for one additional offensive stat

Tier 1 chests drops from all monsters (5% chance)  and contain mostly low level equipments fit for most of the classes, with the lowest range of bonuses - with a very rare chance around 1% (to be changed, it's still the same probability) for Costumes and Mid/Low Headgear pieces

Tier 2 chests drops from monsters lvl 31+ (2,5% chance) and contain better equipments, with a mid range of bonuses - with a chance around 10% (to be changed, it's still the same probability) for Costumes and Mid/Low Headgear pieces

Tier 3 chests drops from monsters lvl 61+ (1,5% chance) and contain high-end equipments + shadow gear with a insane range of bonuses (STR from +10 to +60 for example) - with a chance around 10% (to be changed, it's still the same probability) for EndGame Progression Set Pieces (Grace/Noblesse/Imperial/Illusion/Temporal
